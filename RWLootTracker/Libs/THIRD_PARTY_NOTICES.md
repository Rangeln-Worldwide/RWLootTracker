# Bundled minimap libraries

The following four files were taken unchanged from the official
[LibDBIcon-1.0 v12.1.1 release](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/libdbicon-1-0/files/9119909)
(10 October 2026). They are loaded in this order by RWLootTracker.toc:

- `LibStub/LibStub.lua` (source header places LibStub in the public domain)
- `CallbackHandler-1.0/CallbackHandler-1.0.lua` (Ace3 Development Team)
- `LibDataBroker-1.1/LibDataBroker-1.1.lua` (tekkub)
- `LibDBIcon-1.0/LibDBIcon-1.0.lua` (Funkeh and contributors)

The LibDBIcon [project license](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/libdbicon-1-0/license)
is Ace3 Style BSD. Its [release archive](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/libdbicon-1-0/files/9119909)
contains LibDataBroker and the other dependencies. The Ace3/CallbackHandler
license text is included in `LICENSE-Ace3.txt`. LibDataBroker's
[upstream repository](https://github.com/tekkub/libdatabroker-1-1) and
[project license page](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/libdatabroker-1-1/license)
provide its attribution and terms. The addon's MIT license does not relicense
these files.
